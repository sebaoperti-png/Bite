Bite connects you with home cooks nearby. Share a home-cooked meal, split the cost of the groceries, and pay less than takeaway.

No. 1
The ideaBlaBlaCar for dinner

A neighbour is already cooking tonight. You pull up a chair and pay an equal share of the groceries, not a restaurant price.

No. 2
Three ways to eatyour choice

Pick-up · Eat together at a neighbour's table · Cook together, then eat.

No. 3
A fair pricecost, not profit

Guests pay their share of the ingredients plus a 12% Bite fee, all in the app. No cash, no mark-up, and the host's own dinner gets cheaper too.

No. 4
Safe by designhouse rules

Verified hosts (ID, video call, tasting), small groups, in-app payments, GDPR-first data.

Where we areearly stage

Tested with a survey of 70+ people and interviews; the prototype works. Now we want to hear from you.
